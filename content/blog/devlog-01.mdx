Workbench is currently a Gridfinity-compatible baseplate generator. It runs in the browser, does not require an account, and exports files for printing.

The larger Benchfinity direction is whole-collection orchestration: capture a collection, systemize it, generate the coordinated parts, and print the result. That orchestration layer is roadmap, not shipped.

This site keeps that distinction visible. Present-tense claims describe the baseplate generator. Roadmap claims describe the system Benchfinity is building toward.
